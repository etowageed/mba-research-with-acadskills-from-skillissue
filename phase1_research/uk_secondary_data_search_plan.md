# UK Secondary Dataset & Literature Search Plan (2021–2024)

## 1. UK Quantitative Secondary Data Sources (2021–2024)
| ID | Dataset / Report | Issuing Body | Variables Extracted | Timeframe | Granularity |
|----|------------------|--------------|---------------------|-----------|-------------|
| **DS-1** | Business Insights and Conditions Survey (BICS) — Homeworking & Hybrid Work Waves | Office for National Statistics (ONS) | % of businesses using/intending permanent homeworking/hybrid models; workforce working from home vs. workplace | 2021–2024 | UK SIC 2007 Industry Sections (J, K, M, C, G, I) |
| **DS-2** | Opinions and Lifestyle Survey (OPN) — Characteristics of Homeworkers | ONS | % of working adults hybrid vs. exclusive WFH vs. workplace-only; commute time saved; wellbeing/work-life balance | 2021–2024 | Great Britain workforce, occupation, qualification, sector |
| **DS-3** | Output per Hour Worked, UK (`PRDY` / Labour Productivity Time Series) | ONS | GVA per hour worked (index & % annual change); total hours worked; sectoral productivity growth | 2021–2024 | Whole economy & UK SIC 2007 Sections (J, K, M, C, G, I) |
| **DS-4** | Labour Force Survey (LFS) / Annual Population Survey (APS) — Job-to-Job Moves & Turnover | ONS | Job-to-job resignation/move rates; economic inactivity; hours worked (with explicit 2023–2024 reweighting caveats) | 2021–2024 | UK regions & industry sectors |
| **DS-5** | Flexible and Hybrid Working Practices in the UK (2022, 2023, 2024 Surveys) | Chartered Institute of Personnel and Development (CIPD) | Formal vs. informal hybrid policies; employer-reported productivity & retention impact; attrition due to lack of flexibility | 2021–2024 | UK employers & employees (cross-sector) |
| **DS-6** | Acas / DBT (formerly BEIS) Flexible Working & Employment Relations Evidence | Acas / Department for Business and Trade (UK Gov) | Impact of Employment Relations (Flexible Working) Act 2023 (effective April 2024); day-one request readiness; dispute/retention trends | 2021–2024 | UK employers & regulatory baseline |

## 2. Peer-Reviewed Academic Literature Search Strategy (`deep-research` + `sr-screener`)
- **Databases:** Scopus, Web of Science, Business Source Complete (EBSCO), Google Scholar, ScienceDirect, Emerald Insight.
- **Boolean Search Strings:**
  - `("hybrid work*" OR "flexible work*" OR "telework*" OR "remote work*") AND ("employee retention" OR "turnover intention*" OR "attrition") AND ("productivity" OR "organisational performance" OR "labour productivity") AND ("UK" OR "United Kingdom" OR "sector*")`
  - `("Job Demands-Resources" OR "Social Exchange Theory" OR "Self-Determination Theory" OR "Dynamic Capabilities") AND ("hybrid work*" OR "flexible working")`
- **Timeframe:** 2020–2025 for empirical post-pandemic studies; seminal theoretical works (e.g., Bakker & Demerouti, 2007/2017; Blau, 1964; Deci & Ryan, 2000; Teece et al., 1997) unrestricted by date.

## 3. `sr-screener` Eligibility Protocol (PECO / Inclusion–Exclusion Criteria)
| Criterion | Inclusion | Exclusion |
|-----------|-----------|-----------|
| **Population / Context** | UK organisations, employees, or SIC 2007 industry sectors; comparative OECD/peer-reviewed studies directly informing hybrid-work mechanisms | Purely developing-economy or non-comparable informal-sector studies with no UK/theoretical transferability |
| **Exposure** | Hybrid working arrangements, formal/informal flexible working policies, post-pandemic RTO mandates (2021–2024) | Pre-2020 emergency-only telework without hybrid comparison; gig-economy platform work only |
| **Outcomes** | Quantitative employee retention, turnover/job-to-job moves, labour productivity (GVA/hour, output/worker), or validated organisational performance | Purely anecdotal opinion pieces, non-empirical blog posts, unverified consultancy claims without methodology |
| **Study / Source Type** | Peer-reviewed journal articles (Q1/Q2 ABS-ranked preferred), UK official national statistics (ONS, DBT), authoritative UK professional bodies (CIPD, Acas) | Predatory journals, non-peer-reviewed preprints without verification, paywalled/unverifiable grey literature |
| **Language & Verification** | English; verified DOI, URL, or official ONS dataset identifier | Unverified DOIs, broken/hallucinated citations |
